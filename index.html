Birthday Bash

Turn anyone's life into a playable birthday roast for the TV at the party: chapters of their life, trivia the room shouts at, their catchphrases as running gags, mini-games in between, and an ending that means it.

Build a bash →Play the demo

1 Tell it about them

Name, age, two to four chapters of their life, a few trivia questions, the things they always say, and a pile of photos.

2 Claude writes the show

Every joke, caption and comeback, a painted backdrop and badge per chapter, and ten mini-games reskinned to fit. Endearing first, roast second.

3 Export one file

A single HTML file with the photos inside. Runs offline on the laptop plugged into the TV, or host it so guests play on their phones.
