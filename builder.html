🎂 Birthday Bash
Play the demo

Build a bash

Tell it who the party is for. Claude writes the roast, paints the backdrops and reskins the mini-games. Everything you type stays in this browser until you export.

1 The birthday person

2 Chapters 2 to 4 eras or themes of their life

A title and whatever you remember: places, names, habits, the story everyone tells. The more specific the notes, the better the jokes.

3 Trivia the room shouts the answers

Just the question and the right answer. Claude writes the wrong answers and the punchline, and puts each question in the chapter it fits.

4 -isms the things they always say

These become running gags: wrong-answer buzzers, joke options, button labels, mini-game lines.

5 How hard do we roast?

Whatever the dial says, the last two screens are always sincere.

6 Photos up to 16

Drop them all in. A few words on each ("age 8, county fair") helps a lot. Photos are shrunk in your browser and only leave it if you let Claude look at them.

7 Mini-games one plays after each chapter

Ten games, always the same mechanics; each party just gets new words and emoji. Pick per chapter above, or leave it to Claude. Try any of them with its stock skin:

8 Notes from the room optional

Short messages from guests who want a line in the show. These are shown exactly as written, just before the toast.

9 Write it

The key is sent only to api.anthropic.com, straight from this page. A full show costs roughly the price of a coffee on Opus, less on Sonnet.
